# BRAND — your custom instructions

Free-text house rules for **your** channel. Every `ig-*` skill reads this file before it
plans or builds anything, and **what you write here beats the repo defaults.**

`brand.json` next door holds the machine-readable values (colours, fonts, numbers) that
generate `tokens.css`. This file holds everything that isn't a number: voice, subject
matter, what you refuse to put on screen, how you sign off.

Delete any section you don't care about. An empty section means "use the repo default."
There is no schema here — write like you'd brief a human editor.

---

## Who this is for

<!-- One or two lines. Who's on camera, what the channel is about, who watches it. -->

A creator making short-form vertical video about their own work and systems.

---

## Voice

<!-- How the words should sound on screen. The single most-violated rule is transcribers
     "correcting" a speaker into someone else. Say plainly what must be preserved. -->

- Captions are the speaker's exact words. Keep slang, contractions, double negatives, and
  verbal tics intact. Do **not** formalise or grammar-correct.
- Blunt and spoken, not written. Short sentences.
- ALL-CAPS is a rendering choice for captions and labels — never rewrite copy into caps.

---

## Subject matter

<!-- What the content is actually about. This drives which graphics get chosen: the SHOW
     step reaches for different devices for a money claim vs. a code demo. -->

**Common beats:** numbers and results · before/after comparisons · systems and frameworks
· myths being corrected.

**Reach for the DIGITAL skin when:** the subject is literally computational — code,
tooling, dashboards, build-in-public. Otherwise ANALOG.

---

## Banned on screen

<!-- Hard "never do this" list. These get checked before render. Be specific — a vague ban
     is unenforceable. -->

- Gym mirrors, gym equipment in frame, cars, hotels, watches, luxury/lifestyle b-roll
- Emoji, flying icons, arrow doodles, generic icon-pack glyphs
- Neon / cyan-on-dark / purple-to-blue gradients
- White or light cards over footage
- Bounce, elastic, or back easing
- UI chrome: `@handle` watermarks, "FOLLOW FOR MORE" banners, progress bars, slide counters

---

## Required on screen

<!-- Anything that must appear. Most channels want nothing here — zero chrome is the
     default and usually the right call. Leave empty unless you truly need it. -->

Nothing. Zero UI chrome is the house default.

---

## The CTA

<!-- How videos end. One CTA, always. -->

A single comment-trigger or DM word, in the same caption system. No "link in bio" as well,
no follow banner, no logo. On screen ≥1.8s.

---

## Sound

<!-- Music taste and SFX palette. Name real files if you have a library. -->

- Music: low, moody, cinematic. Ducked ~-18dB under voice, out under the hook line.
- SFX: subtle and premium. One per **visual** event, never per word.
- Banned: airhorns, cartoon boings, stacked risers.

---

## Anything else

<!-- Free space. Recurring notes, pet peeves, things you keep having to correct.
     If you find yourself repeating a note to the agent, write it here instead. -->

- Land graphics on the **word**, not the sentence. One frame late reads as lag.
- Never scale or reposition a rendered graphic in Premiere — fix the composition and
  re-render.
