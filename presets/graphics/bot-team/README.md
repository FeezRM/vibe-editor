# bot-team

"I set up a team of AI agents / they have their own computers / train them like employees / give them tasks" — the
agents shown as a team at work, played out beat by beat in one scene: three desks and computers pop in, a bot drops
into each chair, the team's job types in as a label, the app comes up on every screen and a cursor taps it while the
bots type, disguises (glasses + moustache) go on for "like real people", feedback bubbles pop (icons only), the
screens power up with a flash for "their own computer", onboarding checklists tick, training bars fill over the
heads, ID badges drop onto the chests for "employees", task notes land on the desks and the named task is pinned up
big. Every beat is optional; times are seconds into the graphic. The bots are drawn stand-ins, not a product's
mascot. Placement: `inline` (below the captions, the face stays clear).

Real only: the label and the pinned task are the speaker's words; feedback is icons, never invented quotes.
