# shatter
The word is violent — "OVERPOWERED", "it broke", "destroyed the competition", "that myth is dead": the named
thing lands (an icon from `_icons.html`, a real still, or one short word), strains on `reveal_at[0]` (it shakes
harder and harder, cracks draw out from the centre, a red heat builds), and on `reveal_at[1]` — the stressed
word — it blows apart into triangle shards that spin outward, turn red and burn off. One per video; it is a
punchline, not a transition. Placement: `top` (default) or `inline`.
