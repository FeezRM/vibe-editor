# split
"Your results depend 40% on X, 30% on Y and 30% on Z" — shares of one whole, acted out. Each part (a drawn icon
from `_icons.html` + the speaker's word) slams in on its word; on the whole's noun a ring rises and the parts lift
into orbit; on each spoken number that part dives into the ring and its arc fills, a chip with its share stays on
the arc, and the centre counts to the running total. The whole closes with a flash. Use it when the speaker gives a
breakdown; use `metric` for a single number. The shares are the speaker's claim — `source` says so.
Placement: `inline` (default, below the captions) or `top`.
