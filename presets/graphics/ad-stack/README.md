# ad-stack

"Have you seen these X ads? I see them everywhere" — the ads themselves, piling up: sponsored-post cards (dark mode,
avatar letter, "Sponsored", headline, CTA bar) slam in from alternating sides on the words and land askew on a pile
that sinks and dims under each new one. The last few should come faster ("all the time now"). Frame zero already
shows the first card when its time is 0. Placement: `top` (above the head).

Real only: `brand` is the advertiser, `lines` are the product's real ad or launch copy with provenance in the plan.
No logo is drawn (the avatar is a letter), no invented claims, prices or numbers.
