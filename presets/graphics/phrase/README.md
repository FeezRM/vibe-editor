# phrase

One or two lines of the speaker's **exact words**, hook-sized (108/118 px), one accent word max
per line. It replaces the caption for its duration (`x-mute-captions`), so it must carry the
whole beat on its own. Use it for the opening claim or a payoff line. Wrong when the words are
not the point — a phrase that restates a sentence the caption already shows is decoration.
Needs: the spoken words (from `captions.json`), nothing else.
