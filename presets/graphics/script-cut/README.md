# script-cut
"This video was scripted and edited by …", "I wrote it, then I cut it" — the two verbs acted out on one object.
A page lands and the REAL script writes itself on (a glowing nib at the pen tip); on the edit word one blade
of light slashes it, the page bursts into `clips` strips that fall into a timeline row as clip blocks, the
`remove` ones go red and break apart, the kept ones snap together and an AI spark crosses the row.
Lines are the script's real words (for a talking-head reel the transcript is the script); `clips` is the
EDL's real clip count. Wrong when nothing was written or cut — then it is decoration.
Placement: `top` (above the head, the default) or `inline`.
