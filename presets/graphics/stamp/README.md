# stamp
"all I do is review it", "approved", "that one's a no" — a verdict acted out on the real thing: a playhead runs
the length of it (it was watched), then a ✓ (or ✕ with `mark: cross`) falls out of the air and slams onto the
card, which jolts and glows while a shockwave rings out. Placement: `inline` (default) or `top`.
